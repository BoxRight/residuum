# Pilot 004-R run-009: investigating and committing as separate decisions

## The single change from run-008 (manifest flag `investigation_split`)

One paragraph is added to the explore section of the system prompt
(`contract.diff`):

> During explore, judge hypotheses by how much they can tell you about formal
> consequences and differences. The normative loss does not apply to drafts or
> to their evaluations. Decide separately, at the end of the phase, whether to
> commit any hypothesis; you may commit none. Uncertainty about whether a
> hypothesis is correct is a reason to test it, not a reason to skip the test.

The paragraph corrects a confusion between two operations that the harness
already keeps apart: the hypothetical test (drafts, `evaluate_rule`,
navigation, which are never scored) and the commitment to R_k (scored). It
imposes no hypothesis, no minimum number of consultations and no gate, and
the learner may still commit nothing.

Everything else is run-008: the planning step, the draft contract, the
findings reports, tools, budgets, loss, corpus, valuations, model and
effort. The flag only applies together with the draft contract.

Motivation (run-007 and run-008, literal): the explore abstentions justify
skipping tests by the future loss, for example *"Any extra rule is speculative
and could add excluded positions. I will wait for the scoring feedback
first."* That holds even though drafts and their evaluations are never
scored.
