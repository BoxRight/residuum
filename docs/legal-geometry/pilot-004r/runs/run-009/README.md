# Pilot 004-R run-009: investigating and committing as separate decisions

## The single change from run-008 (manifest flag `investigation_split`)

One paragraph is added to the explore section of the system prompt
(`contract.diff`):

> During explore, judge hypotheses by how much they can tell you about formal
> consequences and differences. The normative loss does not apply to drafts or
> to their evaluations. Decide separately, at the end of the phase, whether to
> commit any hypothesis; you may commit none. Uncertainty about whether a
> hypothesis is correct is a reason to test it, not a reason to skip the test.

The paragraph corrects a confusion between two operations that the harness
already keeps apart: the hypothetical test (drafts, `evaluate_rule`,
navigation, which are never scored) and the commitment to R_k (scored). It
imposes no hypothesis, no minimum number of consultations and no gate, and
the learner may still commit nothing.

Everything else is run-008: the planning step, the draft contract, the
findings reports, tools, budgets, loss, corpus, valuations, model and
effort. The flag only applies together with the draft contract.

Motivation (run-007 and run-008, literal): the explore abstentions justify
skipping tests by the future loss, for example *"Any extra rule is speculative
and could add excluded positions. I will wait for the scoring feedback
first."* That holds even though drafts and their evaluations are never
scored.

## Result (paid run, `run/`)

11 requests, US$0.175, prequential loss 5 (B7–B11 missed on Brookside, as in
run-003 to run-008). h1 is committed in the Brookside revise and stays
committed. No evaluations, navigation, questions or findings.

### What changed

For the first time in runs 003 to 009, the learner drafts a hypothesis in the
Brookside explore phase, before any feedback. That hypothesis is h1
(`obligation ⇒ right`, same subject), and the rationale reads *"Reciprocity:
obligation yields a corresponding right (creditor side)."* In run-003 to
run-008, h1 always originated in the Brookside revise phase.

### What did not change

- **The draft was never tested.** The next turn (0 thinking tokens) ends the
  phase: *"Uncertain; commit nothing to avoid excluded positions."* The
  abstention reads *"speculative rights rule is untested against documented
  positions, so I commit nothing."* The learner names the missing test as its
  reason not to commit, without running it, although the instruction said
  that uncertainty is a reason to test.
- **The plan was not executed.** The Brookside plan listed `inspect_theory`,
  `evaluate_rule`, `compare_partition` and `begin_residual`, and the plan
  trace shows `planned_not_executed` for all four. The plan also named the
  alternative with subject and beneficiary swapped (*"obligation(id,s,c,o,b,...)
  gives right(id,b,c,o,s,...)"*, the h2 form), but the drafted rule kept the
  same subject. The alternative was never drafted, so it was never compared.
- **The decision to commit came from feedback.** In revise: *"h1 yields
  exactly those rights (same subject jant). Commit."*
- **Casa Fiesta and Flowers.** The learner abstains in explore with no
  operation; these dossiers have 0 valued positions and 10 unreachable
  excluded positions respectively. The Casa Fiesta abstention cites loss
  again: *"an excluded derived position would add loss."*

### Reading

The paragraph moved the draft forward (proposal before feedback). It did not
produce testing, comparison or alternative drafts. As in run-008, the plans
are investigable but almost none of their operations are executed (1 of 10
planned operations across the three dossiers, the Brookside `propose_rule`;
no consultation was executed). The decision to stop is taken in short
turns with no reasoning tokens.
